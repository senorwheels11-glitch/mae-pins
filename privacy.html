Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MaeRhysCo is a small online shop that sells digital clipart on Etsy. This page explains how the MaeRhysCo scheduling tool, a personal tool used only by the shop owner, handles data.

Who uses the tool

The tool is for personal use by the shop owner only. It is not offered to the public, and no one else is given access to it.

What data it handles

The tool connects to the shop owner's own Pinterest account to create and schedule Pins that promote MaeRhysCo listings. It reads and writes only the owner's own Pins and boards. It does not collect, store or share data about other Pinterest users.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the shop owner's own computer and are used only to publish the owner's own Pins. They can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account settings.

Shop customers

Purchases are handled entirely by Etsy. MaeRhysCo does not collect payment details or personal information beyond what Etsy provides to sellers to fulfil an order. See Etsy's privacy policy for how Etsy handles customer data.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the shop on Etsy: etsy.com/shop/MaeRhysCo.
